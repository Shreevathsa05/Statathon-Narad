**Survey Blueprint – Household Consumption Study**  \n*(Based on the **Household Consumption Expenditure Survey (HCES)** indicators returned in Step 2)*  \n\n---\n\n## 1. Study Overview  \n\n| Item | Detail |\n|------|--------|\n| **Dataset** | **HCES** – Household Consumption Expenditure Survey (MoSPI) |\n| **Purpose** | Capture detailed patterns of monthly per‑capita consumption, its distribution across households, and inequality (Gini). |\n| **Target Population** | All private households in India (urban + rural) covered by the HCES frame. |\n| **Sampling Frame** | 100 % of households (census‑type) – the survey already provides state‑level, fractile‑class, and social‑group breakdowns. |\n| **Survey Length** | ~ 30 minutes per interview (≈ 15 – 20 questions). |\n| **Key Outputs** | • Average monthly per‑capita consumption expenditure (MPCE) <br>• MPCE by 12 fractile classes <br>• MPCE by broad consumption categories (food, health, education, etc.) <br>• MPCE by household type & social group <br>• Gini coefficient for consumption inequality |\n\n---\n\n## 2. Indicator Reference (HCES)\n\n| Indicator Code | Description (as per MoSPI) | Typical Use in a Consumption Survey |\n|----------------|----------------------------|--------------------------------------|\n| **1** | Average monthly per‑capita consumption expenditure (MPCE) | Core metric – overall consumption level |\n| **2** | MPCE over 12 Fractile Classes | Shows consumption distribution across income/wealth groups |\n| **3** | Monthly Per‑Capita Consumption Expenditure over Broad Categories in 30 days | Breaks consumption down into major spending groups (food, health, education, etc.) |\n| **4** | Percentage Share of MPCE over Broad Categories in 30 days | Provides share of each category in total consumption |\n| **5** | Average Per‑Capita Monthly Quantity Consumption | Quantity (kg, litres, units) consumed – useful for physical‑goods analysis |\n| **6** | Average Monthly Per‑Capita Value of Consumption (Rs.) | Monetary value per capita – complements quantity data |\n| **7** | MPCE across different Household Types | Consumption by type: nuclear, joint, single‑parent, etc. |\n| **8** | MPCE across different Social Groups | Consumption by caste/tribe, religion, SC/ST/OBC/General |\n| **9** | Gini Coefficient for Per‑Capita Consumption Expenditure | Inequality measure – a single summary statistic |\n\n*All nine indicators are available at the **state**, **year**, **fractile class**, **household type**, and **social‑group** levels (see Step 3 for the exact filter codes).*\n\n---\n\n## 3. Survey Design – Question Flow  \n\nBelow is a **modular questionnaire** that can be administered electronically (e‑survey) or on paper.  \nEach question is
